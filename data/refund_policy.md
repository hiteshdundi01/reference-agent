# Acme Store refund policy

## Section 1: Who this covers

This policy covers refunds on orders placed at Acme Store by a customer with an account. A refund goes back to the payment method used for the order.

## Section 2: The refund window

A customer may ask for a refund within 30 days of delivery, except for faulty goods, which Section 3 covers for 90 days. A request outside its window is declined, unless Section 6 applies.

## Section 3: Damaged or faulty goods

Goods that arrive damaged, or are faulty within 90 days of delivery, are refunded in full, including shipping. The customer's description of the damage or fault is enough for an order of $100 or less. Above $100, the request needs a photo or a carrier's damage report, as the request's evidence says. Without one, escalate.

## Section 4: Change of mind

Unused goods returned within the refund window are refunded at their price, less shipping. Goods marked final sale aren't refunded for a change of mind.

## Section 5: Goods that never arrived

An order the carrier shows as lost, or that hasn't arrived 10 days after its expected delivery date, is refunded in full. An order the carrier shows as delivered is escalated, not declined.

## Section 6: Exceptions

A supervisor may approve a refund outside this policy for a good reason, such as a long-standing customer or our own mistake. The agent never makes an exception itself. It escalates a request that might deserve one.

## Section 7: Repeated requests

A customer with three or more refunds in the last 12 months is escalated, whatever the reason, so that a person looks at the pattern.

## Section 8: How much

A refund is never more than what the customer paid for the order. A refund for part of an order is the price of the items concerned.

## Section 9: The decision record

Every decision records the decision, the amount for an approval, the section of this policy it relies on, the reason in two or three sentences, who or what made it, and when.
